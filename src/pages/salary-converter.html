Hourly to salary converter

Turn an hourly rate into a yearly salary, or a salary into an hourly rate. Australia's standard full-time week is 38 hours.

The conversions

FromTo annual pay
Hourly× hours per week × 52
Weekly× 52
Fortnightly× 26
Monthly× 12

Monthly pay is a yearly figure divided by 12, not four weekly pays. A month averages about 4.33 weeks, so "weekly × 4" understates monthly income by around 8%.

Worked example: $35 an hour, full time

$35 × 38 hours = $1,330 a week. Over 52 weeks that is $69,160 a year, or $2,660 a fortnight and $5,763.33 a month, all before tax. Paid annual leave is already counted because permanent staff are paid for all 52 weeks.

Casual vs permanent

Most awards pay casuals a 25% loading instead of paid annual leave, personal leave and notice. To compare a casual job with a permanent one, divide the casual rate by 1.25. A $40/hour casual rate is equivalent to a $32/hour permanent rate before you account for the leave you won't be paid for.

Which is better depends on how steady the hours are. A permanent job pays the same in a week you are sick or on holiday; a casual job pays nothing. Four weeks of annual leave plus ten days of personal leave is worth about 13% of a permanent salary, so on paper the 25% loading pays more. Permanent work makes up the gap with steady hours, notice and redundancy rights, which matter most if your casual hours could be cut without warning.

Checking your rate

The national minimum wage and award rates are set by the Fair Work Commission and usually rise on 1 July each year. The Fair Work Ombudsman's Pay Calculator gives the legal minimum for your award, age and classification; if this converter shows you are being paid less, check there first.
