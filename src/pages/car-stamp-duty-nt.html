Northern Territory car stamp duty calculator

Stamp duty charged when a motor vehicle registration is issued or transferred in the NT, collected by the Motor Vehicle Registry (MVR).

{{ad}}

NT stamp duty rate

Duty = $3 for every $100, or part of $100, of the dutiable value. One flat 3% rate for every value, with no steps.

The NT is the simplest state to work out and, for a car above about $45,000, one of the cheapest, because there is no higher tier for dearer cars.

Electric vehicle concession to 30 June 2027

From 1 July 2022 until 30 June 2027 the NT gives plug-in electric vehicles a stamp duty concession of up to $1,500, the duty on the first $50,000:

  • A battery electric car or plug-in hybrid worth up to $50,000 pays no stamp duty.
  • Above $50,000 you pay 3% on the amount over $50,000 only.
  • It covers new and used vehicles of any age, cars owned by companies, business vehicles and heavy vehicles, and it is applied automatically when you register or transfer.
  • The vehicle must have an electric primary power source and be recorded as BEV or PHEV on the registration certificate or roadworthy inspection. A regular hybrid that cannot plug in does not qualify.
  • Eligible plug-in vehicles also get free registration over the same period, though the Motor Accidents Compensation charge still applies.

What value is the duty charged on?

Usually the sale price, which includes accessories added to the car, any trade-in allowance and dealer delivery charges. In some cases, such as a sale between family members that is not at arm's length, the market value is used instead.

What this leaves out

  • Registration fees and the Motor Accidents Compensation (MAC) charge.
  • Exemptions, which need the Territory Revenue Office's application form.
  • Changes after 30 June 2027, when the EV concession is due to end.
