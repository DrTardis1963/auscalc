About {{site_name}}

{{site_name}} is a small set of free calculators for everyday Australian money questions: what you take home after tax, what a loan or a road trip will cost, whether solar or an electric car pays off. It is made by Prime Mover Industries, an Australian independent developer.

Where the numbers come from

Tax, Medicare levy, tax offset and super figures come from the Australian Taxation Office's published rates. Each calculator page names its sources and the date they were last checked. Rates are reviewed at least once a year, after each Federal Budget and before 1 July, when most of them change.

Where a figure varies too much to publish one official number, such as electricity prices or solar output, the calculator uses a typical value and lets you type in your own. Your bill, quote or payslip will always be more accurate than any average.

How the calculators are checked

The formulas behind every calculator are kept in one place and tested against hand-worked examples, including the examples printed on each page. If an example on a page and the calculator ever disagree, that's a bug: please tell us.

Privacy

The calculators run entirely in your browser. What you type in is not sent to us or stored. See the privacy policy for details about cookies and advertising.

Not advice

These calculators give general information and estimates. They don't know your full situation and are not financial, tax or legal advice. For decisions that matter, check with the ATO, your lender, your super fund or a licensed financial adviser.
