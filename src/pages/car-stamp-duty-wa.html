Western Australia car stamp duty calculator

Vehicle licence duty, paid to the Department of Transport when a vehicle is licensed or a licence is transferred into your name. For most cars priced between $50,000 and $100,000, WA's duty is the highest in Australia.

For light vehicles: 4.5 tonnes gross mass or less.

Over $50,0006.5% of the value

The rate always applies to the whole value. Between $25,000 and $50,000 the rate itself climbs by 0.15 percentage points for every $1,000, from 2.75% to 6.5%, so the duty rises much faster than the price: going from $40,000 to $50,000 adds $1,250 of duty.

What value is the duty charged on?

  • New car: the retail selling price set by the manufacturer for Western Australia, including the factory options such as transmission and engine size. A delivery or order fee built into the manufacturer's price counts; charges a dealer adds on top do not.
  • Used car: you estimate the value on the Notification of Change of Ownership form. RevenueWA's practice note DA 50 explains how, and it can reassess a value that is too low.
  • Demonstrators: a demo car must have been used for more than two months before it can be sold as used. A car that only sat in the showroom is still new and pays duty on the full retail price.
  • Light commercial vehicles: the open-market value on the date of transfer, using Redbook as a guide.

Electric and hybrid cars

WA's duty rates do not change with the fuel: an EV, a hybrid and a petrol car with the same dutiable value pay the same duty. For an EV the manufacturer's fixed price, including any delivery or order fee it sets, is the value.

What this leaves out

  • The vehicle licence fee and compulsory third-party insurance, which are separate.
  • Exemptions: transfers between spouses or de facto partners, cars returned for a refund or a cancelled purchase, some concession card holders, wheelchair-hoist vehicles and classic cars. Some transfers pay a nominal $20 instead.
  • A car last registered in your own name in another state, which pays no duty when you license it in WA.
  • Heavy vehicles: 3% of the value, capped at $12,000 of duty.
