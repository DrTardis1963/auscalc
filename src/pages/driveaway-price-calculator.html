Drive-away price calculator

The price on a new car's sticker is rarely what you pay. This adds the dealer's delivery charge, stamp duty for your state and a year of registration and CTP insurance, and shows each line so you can check a dealer's quote.

What goes into a drive-away price

PartWho sets itCan you negotiate it?
Car price and optionsManufacturer and dealerYes
Dealer deliveryDealerYes, often
Stamp dutyYour state, by a fixed formulaNo, but the price and options it is charged on can change
Registration and CTP insuranceYour state; CTP by the insurer within limitsOnly by choosing the CTP insurer

So when you compare quotes, the government part should be the same at every dealer in your state for the same car. If two quotes differ, the difference is in the price, options or dealer delivery.

How registration is charged in each state

Every state charges rego differently, which is why no single figure fits the whole country:

  • Queensland: by number of cylinders, plus a flat traffic improvement fee and CTP. QLD rego calculator.
  • South Australia: by cylinders and whether you live in the metro area or the country; the published fees include a CTP estimate. SA fees.
  • Victoria: a registration fee plus the TAC charge, which depends on where the car is kept: $958.80 a year in Melbourne, $897.10 in outer metro areas and $824.60 in the country for a car or SUV. VicRoads fees.
  • New South Wales: a registration fee plus motor vehicle tax by the car's weight (tare), and a separate green slip (CTP) bought from an insurer. Service NSW calculator.
  • Western Australia: a licence fee by the car's gross vehicle mass, a recording fee and motor injury insurance. Transport WA fees.
  • ACT: based on the car's emissions, weight and use, plus compulsory MAI insurance. Access Canberra fees.
  • Tasmania and the Northern Territory: use the state's own registration quote, then type the amount into the calculator above.

Common questions

Is luxury car tax included?

Enter the list price including luxury car tax if the car has it. Luxury car tax is a federal tax built into the price of dearer cars; this calculator does not add it on top.

Why is my dealer's quote higher?

Check the dealer delivery line and any extras such as paint protection, tinting or extended warranties. The duty and rego should match this calculator within a few dollars for the same car and state.

Does the duty use the price I negotiated?

It depends on the state. In Queensland, a new car's duty is charged on its list price plus options, so a discount does not lower it. Other states' pages explain their rules.

Is a used car's price "drive-away"?

Usually not. A used car often comes with the seller's remaining rego, and the buyer pays the transfer fee and stamp duty. Use your state's stamp duty calculator for the duty.

What this leaves out

  • Business-use and novated-lease cars, fleet discounts and primary producer concessions.
  • Concession card rates for rego (see the QLD rego calculator for Queensland).
  • Rego for utes and vans in SA and VIC, which is charged by weight or type: enter it yourself.
  • Finance costs, insurance beyond CTP, and roadside assistance.
